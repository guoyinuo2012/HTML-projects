Take your calendar with you

.ics supports events and daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly rules. Custom intervals, weekly day selections, and excluded dates are supported. Other complex rules are skipped and reported. Backups preserve all app settings and tasks.

About this workspace

Your events are saved in this browser. Export a backup to move to another device. This is an independent personal calendar: Google sync, emailed invitations and RSVPs, Meet creation, shared calendars, room booking, public booking pages, Gmail events, holidays feeds, and Google’s AI features are not connected. Focus time, working location, and out-of-office are local event labels; they do not decline invitations automatically.
